## 📋 About POSH9JA

**POSH9JA Export & Logistics** is a Lagos-based international trade and logistics partner for Nigerian businesses, families, and SMEs. We simplify global trade by providing reliable, affordable, and efficient export and logistics solutions.

### Mission
To simplify global trade for Nigerian businesses by providing reliable, affordable, and efficient export and logistics solutions.

### Vision
To become Africa's leading export and logistics hub, connecting local producers to international markets seamlessly.

### Core Values
- **Integrity** — Transparent pricing, no hidden charges
- **Efficiency** — Fast clearance, weekly loadings
- **Innovation** — Real-time tracking, digital documentation
- **Customer-Centricity** — Diaspora-first service approach

## 🚀 Services

### 📦 Export Solutions
- **Agricultural Produce** — Dried goods, spices, cassava products
- **Frozen Foods** — Ponmo, goat meat, stockfish, egusi
- **Household Goods** — Furniture, electronics, personal items
- **Business Cargo** — Raw materials, manufactured goods, auto parts
- **Personal Shopping** — Source and ship items on behalf of diaspora clients

### 🚚 Logistics Services
- **Freight Forwarding** — Air & Sea (DDP/DDU/EXW/FOB)
- **Customs Clearance** — NEPC, HS codes, documentation
- **Warehousing** — Secure storage in Lagos and destination cities
- **Consolidation** — Combine multiple shipments for cost savings
- **Last-Mile Delivery** — Door-to-door from warehouse to doorstep

### 🔧 Value-Added Services
- **Professional Packing** — Export-grade, sand-free, shelf-ready
- **Insurance Coverage** — Full and partial cargo protection
- **Documentation Support** — Form M, NXP, invoices, packing lists, COO
- **Real-Time Tracking** — SMS/email notifications at every stage
- **Amazon FBA Prep** — Ready-to-shelf packaging for online sellers
